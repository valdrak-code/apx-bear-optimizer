📖 APX Field Guide

Welcome to the Bear Hunt Optimizer

The APX Bear Hunt Optimizer helps you build stronger Bear Hunt formations by analyzing your troop counts, maximum marches, rally cap, and Rally Leader settings.

The goal is simple: maximize damage, minimize waste, and explain why.

1. Enter Your Troops

Use the troop counts from your Squad tab.

  • 🏹 Total Archers — your primary Bear Hunt damage troop.
  • 🐎 Total Cavalry — used to fill Formation space efficiently.
  • 🛡️ Total Infantry — kept low when possible, usually near the preferred 10% target.

Use your full troop totals. Bear Hunt planning assumes marches are recalled and available for the event.

2. Set Your Bear Hunt Settings

  • Maximum Marches — the most joiner marches your account can send.
  • Alliance Rally Cap Limit — the max troops APX allows per joiner rally.
  • Check if you are a Rally Leader — turn this on if you will launch your own rally.
  • Rally Leader March Size — your maximum march size when launching a rally.

3. Choose Your Optimization Strategy

⚔️ Prioritize Joiners

Best for most players. The optimizer focuses on your joiner marches first, then uses remaining troops for Rally Leader if enabled.

👑 Prioritize Rally Leader

Best when launching your own rally is the priority. The optimizer reserves the Rally Leader Formation first, then builds joiners from what remains.

⚖️ Balanced

Attempts to avoid sacrificing either side too heavily. This is useful when you want a solid Rally Leader Formation and useful joiner output.

4. Press LET'S GOOO!

The optimizer evaluates multiple possible Formations, scores them, then selects the best recommendation based on your settings.

The button also jumps you to the APX Recommended Setup so you can quickly see what to send.

5. Copy Your Recommendation

Use 📋 Copy Recommendation to copy a Discord-friendly version of your setup.

This is useful if someone asks what APX Tools recommended or if you want to compare setups with alliance members.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why didn't APX Tools recommend all of my marches?

More marches are not always better. If extra marches make each Formation too small or too weak, the optimizer may recommend fewer stronger marches.

Why is Cavalry being substituted?

Cavalry is used to fill unused Formation space after Archer allocation. It helps get closer to the Alliance Rally Cap Limit without overusing Infantry.

Why isn't Infantry exactly 10%?

The 10% Infantry rule is a preferred target, not an absolute hard rule. The optimizer may adjust when troop counts are limited.

What does “Playable, but Not Optimal” mean?

The setup can be used, but APX Tools found limitations that may reduce Bear Hunt damage. The APX Recommended Setup is usually preferred.

Why are my results different from another player?

Recommendations are based on your troop counts, max marches, Rally Leader setting, Rally Leader size, alliance cap, and selected strategy.
