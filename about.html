ℹ️ About

Why APX Tools Exists

APX Tools was created to remove the guesswork from Bear Hunt formations. The goal is to help players make stronger, faster decisions without needing spreadsheets, manual math, or trial and error.

What started as a simple formation calculator evolved into the APX Optimization Engine: a tool designed to maximize damage, minimize waste, and explain why each recommendation was made.

Built for Bear Hunt

The Bear Hunt Optimizer analyzes your troop counts, maximum marches, alliance rally cap, Rally Leader settings, and optimization strategy to recommend practical formations.

It is not trying to create perfect math on paper. It is trying to create formations that make sense in real gameplay.

Created by Valdrak

APX Tools was created by Valdrak for APX / Apex Predators.

The optimizer has been shaped through actual player testing, real alliance feedback, and repeated tuning based on how APX members use the tool.

Design Philosophy

Maximize Damage

Recommendations are built around practical Bear Hunt performance, not just the largest possible march.

Minimize Waste

The optimizer attempts to use available troops efficiently while respecting Rally Leader needs and alliance limits.

Explain Why

Every result includes a report, coaching note, and insight so players understand the recommendation.

Community Refined

Every major improvement came from real testing: Maximum Marches, Rally Leader logic, Cavalry fill, Infantry soft cap, Formation cards, APX Coach, and Copy Recommendation.

Independent Fan Project

APX Tools is an independent fan-created utility for entertainment and gameplay planning purposes.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by KingShot or its developers.
